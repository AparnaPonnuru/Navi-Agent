## 🔍 WHAT THE SYSTEM CURRENTLY CHECKS (Built-in Accuracy Controls)

### ✅ 1. Readiness Score (Rule-Based — Accurate)
The readiness score is **not AI** — it's calculated by fixed rules:

| Grade | Readiness Score | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Grade 12 | **80** | Advanced Starter |
| Grade 11 | **65** | Intermediate Starter |
| Grade 10 | **55** | Intermediate Starter |
| Grade 9 or below | **35** | Early Starter |
| Competitive Goal | −10 to −15 points deducted | |

**Accuracy: ~90%** — Very reliable since it's rule-based, not AI guesswork.

---

### ✅ 2. Blind Spots Detection (Semi-Accurate)
The system currently generates generic blind spots like:
- *"Lacks formal international exposure - needs IELTS/SAT preparation."*
- *"Needs structured extracurricular profile development."*

**Accuracy: ~50%** — Currently these are hardcoded templates, not dynamically analyzed.
**Improvement needed:** Should be generated by AI based on the actual profile.

---

### ✅ 3. Step Content (AI-Generated — Variable Accuracy)
Agent 1 (the Blueprint Agent) uses the **Llama 3.3-70B** model to generate:
- Step titles and descriptions
- Macro / Micro / Nano views
- Marketplace vendors

**Accuracy factors:**
- More **specific profile** = More accurate output
- More **specific goal** = More accurate output
- Vague inputs → Generic output

## 🧪 HOW TO CHECK THE ACCURACY YOURSELF

### TEST 1 — Check Vendor Names in Marketplace
Go to any step → Open Marketplace → Vendors tab.
Manually verify:

| What to Check | How |
|---|---|
| Is the vendor name real? | Google the vendor name |
| Does it match the goal? | E.g. Coursera for AI — ✅ relevant |
| Is the cost accurate? | Check vendor website directly |
| Is "Free audit" claim true? | Coursera does offer free audits — ✅ |

**Common accurate vendors the system generates:**
- Coursera ✅, edX ✅, Khan Academy ✅, freeCodeCamp ✅, Kaggle ✅
- MIT OpenCourseWare ✅, YouTube ✅, Google Developers ✅

**Watch out for:**
- AI may invent plausible-sounding but fake vendor names
- Always verify vendor names with a quick Google search

---

### TEST 2 — Check Timeline / Duration Accuracy
Total duration on the pathway should match your grade-to-university timeline.

| Grade | Expected Timeline to University |
|---|---|
| Grade 10 | ~24–36 months (2–3 years) |
| Grade 11 | ~12–18 months (1–1.5 years) |
| Grade 12 | ~6–12 months |

**How to check:**
- Look at the "Duration" badge on the top right of the pathway
- Add up all step durations (e.g., "4 months + 6 months + 3 months...")
- If total feels too short or too long, use Refine: *"make the timeline more realistic for a Grade 10 student"*

---

### TEST 3 — Check Step Relevance
Read each step title and ask:
1. ✅ Is this step logically ordered? (Foundation → Advanced)
2. ✅ Does each step lead toward the final goal?
3. ✅ Are the steps achievable in the given duration?
4. ❌ Are there missing critical steps? (e.g., no SAT prep for US university goal?)

---

### TEST 4 — Check Marketplace Vendor Links
The system currently **does NOT verify vendor URLs** — links are not included.
To add accuracy checking for vendors, you would need to manually:
1. Google the vendor name
2. Check if they offer what the system says
3. Verify pricing/free tier claims

---

### TEST 5 — Compare 3 Pathway Options
The system generates 3 alternatives:
- **Academic & Research** — for exam-focused students
- **Practical & Industry** — for skill/project builders
- **Holistic & Career-Prep** — for well-rounded applicants

**Check:** Does each alternative feel genuinely different? If all 3 look the same, the AI may have generated generic content.
