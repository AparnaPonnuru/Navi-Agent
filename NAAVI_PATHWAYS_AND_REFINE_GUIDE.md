# 🗺️ Naavi Agent — Pathway Types & Refine Guide

This guide details the differences between the **three alternative pathway types** generated by the Naavi Path Engine and provides reference templates for using the **Refine with Naavi Agent** feature.

---

## 🗂️ 1. The Three Alternative Pathway Types

When you input a starting position and a future goal, the Naavi AI engine generates three distinct pathways. Although they all lead to the same destination, each pathway represents a different preparation philosophy:

### 🎓 A. Academic & Research
* **Target Audience**: Students aiming for highly competitive international universities (e.g., Ivy League, Oxbridge, top-tier research institutes) where academic credentials are the primary gatekeeper.
* **Core Areas**:
  * Achieving top grades (e.g., 90%+ in board/school exams).
  * High-stakes standardized testing preparation (SAT, ACT, IELTS, TOEFL).
  * Research paper preparation, scoping academic projects, and selecting rigorous curriculum streams.
* **Philosophy**: Prove capability through traditional academic rigor, exam results, and intellectual depth.

### 💻 B. Practical & Industry
* **Target Audience**: Students who learn best by doing, want to build software/products, or plan to transition directly into industry roles.
* **Core Areas**:
  * Technical/hands-on skill acquisition (learning programming languages, libraries, and frameworks).
  * Portfolio development (publishing code to GitHub, building deployed applications).
  * Earning recognized industry certifications (e.g., AWS, Cisco, developer credentials).
  * Participating in hackathons and securing industry internships.
* **Philosophy**: Prove capability by showcasing a robust portfolio of built projects and practical experience.

### 🤝 C. Holistic & Career-Prep
* **Target Audience**: Students aiming for well-rounded profiles that showcase leadership, adaptability, soft skills, and community engagement.
* **Core Areas**:
  * Soft skills competency (public speaking, writing, presentation design).
  * Extracurricular activities, peer cohort campaigns, and community service.
  * Counselor reviews, mock behavioral interviews, and career diagnostics.
* **Philosophy**: Prove capability by showing emotional intelligence (EQ), communication power, and active group collaboration.

## 🛠️ 2. How the Refine Section Works

Naavi features a **Smart Refine Router** that lets you update details dynamically without regenerating the whole roadmap.

### 🔄 Surgical vs. Full Refinement
* **Surgical Update (Recommended)**: If you specify a target step (e.g., *"update step 3..."*), the AI will isolate and patch **only that step's specific field**, leaving the rest of the roadmap untouched.
* **Full Pathway Update**: If you specify general path changes (e.g., *"make the timeline suitable for a Grade 10 student"*), the AI will regenerate the entire pathway structure.

## 📌 Field Keyword Cheat Sheet (Surgical Patches)

To make a surgical update, include the word **"step" followed by the step number** and use one of the target keywords below:

| Target Component | Keywords to Include in Prompt | Example Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| **Step Description** | `description`, `desc`, `summary` | *“update the description for step 1 to focus on board exams”* |
| **Macro View** | `macro view`, `macro overview`, `macro tab` | *“rewrite the macro view for step 2 with more academic detail”* |
| **Micro View** | `micro view`, `micro tab`, `detailed view` | *“make the micro view in step 3 more practical”* |
| **Nano View** | `nano view`, `nano tab`, `expert view` | *“update the nano view for step 4 to mention AI tutors”* |
| **Micro Steps** | `checklist`, `todo`, `micro steps`, `tasks` | *“update the checklist for step 1 with SAT prep tasks”* |
| **Free Resources** | `vendors`, `marketplace`, `free resources`, `market` | *“update the marketplace vendors for step 1 with Coursera”* |
| **Paid Courses** | `paid courses`, `certification`, `bootcamp` | *“update the paid courses in the marketplace for step 2”* |
| **Mentors & Coaches** | `mentor`, `coaching`, `provider` | *“update the mentor recommendations in step 3”* |

## ⚠️ Important Rules for Prompting
1. **Always Use Numbers**: Use `"step 1"` instead of `"step one"`.
2. **Be Specific**: Avoid vague prompts like *"make it better."* Tell the AI exactly what to add, delete, or rewrite.
3. **Wait for Validation**: After typing, wait for the Naavi Agent avatar to show the green ✅ **"Instruction looks good!"** message before clicking the button.
